Commonveil is a place to talk: one-to-one, in small groups, and in larger communities called servers, with voice and video calls. Everything you say is **end-to-end encrypted**. Your messages are locked on your device before they leave it and unlocked only on the devices of the people you're talking to. Commonveil stores the locked copies so they can be delivered, but it has no way to read them.

## What makes it different

- **Nobody can browse for you.** There's no member directory and no "people you may know". Someone can only find you by typing your exact username.
- **Collects only what it needs.** No phone number, no contacts, no location, no adverts, no trackers. The [privacy report](/help/privacy-report) shows exactly what is held about you.
- **Your keys stay with you.** Your private keys are made on your device and never leave your devices. A stolen password isn't enough to read your messages or take over your account.
- **Servers without a server that can read them.** Channels, roles, threads, events, emoji and moderation, all encrypted, with member lists that only show people who choose to be listed.
- **Features built for privacy and safety:** [disappearing messages and self-destructing conversations](/help/disappearing-and-self-destruct), [send later and safety check-ins](/help/send-later-and-check-ins), [quiet hours](/help/notifications), a passcode lock, and [safety numbers](/help/safety-numbers) to prove nobody is in the middle.
- **Protected against tomorrow's computers too.** Keys are sealed with a post-quantum hybrid (ML-KEM-768 with P-256), so traffic recorded today stays protected against a future quantum computer.

## Where to start

1. [Create your account](/help/create-account) and write down your 24-word recovery phrase.
2. [Start a conversation](/help/conversations) with someone whose username you know.
3. [Compare safety numbers](/help/safety-numbers) with the people who matter most.
4. Run the [privacy checkup](/help/privacy-report) to tighten the rest.

## An honest note

Commonveil is working and tested, but its encryption hasn't yet been independently audited. Until a review is published, don't rely on it where someone's safety depends on it. [How the encryption works](/help/how-encryption-works) explains what is and isn't protected.
